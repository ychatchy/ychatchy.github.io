Privacy Policy / プライバシーポリシー

Effective date / 施行日: 2026-10-07

English

  • Data accessed: Gmail and Google Drive data of the owner (Yuta Chatani), using read-only scopes only. The app never modifies, sends, or deletes anything.
  • Use: The data is shown only to the owner, through his own AI assistant.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's Mac only. No data is stored on any external server.
  • Sharing: Data is never shared, sold, or transferred to anyone. No humans other than the owner access the data.
  • Deletion: Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the local tokens can be deleted.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy: Yuta Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • 保存: 認証トークンは所有者の Mac 内にのみ保存します。外部サーバーには保存しません。
  • 共有: データを第三者に共有・販売・譲渡しません。所有者以外の人がデータを見ることはありません。
  • 削除: myaccount.google.com/permissions でいつでもアクセス権を取り消せます。ローカルのトークンも削除できます。
